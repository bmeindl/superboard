# [Role name]

**Purpose and reader.** Who uses the result, and what decision or task should it help them finish?

**Inputs.** Which project folder or documents should this role read? What evidence can it rely on?

**Authority.** What may it change, whom may it ask for help, and which actions require the user's decision? Configure permitted handover recipients in crew/crew.json. The runner's host permissions apply.

**Method.** Write the two or three steps that matter most for a good result.

**Return.** What should arrive on the owner's card? How should missing information or a failed check be reported?

**First trial.** Give a realistic small task and name something observable that distinguishes a good answer.
