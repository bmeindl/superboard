## Support question

A customer asks whether their team can search attachments today. Write a short release note suitable for answering them.

## Release record

- Finding a notebook by its title is available to everyone.
- Searching the text of attachments is in preview. Only workspace owners who opted into the preview have access.
- Preview search indexes PDF attachments under 10 MB. Scanned pages without a text layer are skipped.
